Research internship

Mar – Aug 2026 Institut Pascal · ISPR / PerSyst, Clermont-Ferrand

Benchmarking and improving a multi-robot collaborative SLAM framework

Built a reproducible benchmarking pipeline for LiDAR-based collaborative SLAM: run public datasets through each C-SLAM framework, evaluate the estimated trajectories against ground truth with evo (APE / RPE), and compare. On the GRACO dataset I evaluated DiSCo-SLAM, Swarm-SLAM, and LAMP 2.0. DiSCo-SLAM was the most reliable, with roughly 15 times lower trajectory error than Swarm-SLAM, while the centralized LAMP 2.0 showed a map-fusion failure. Supervised by Dr. P. Checchin and Dr. A. K. Aijazi.

About

I am a control engineering graduate from ENP, finishing an M2 in Advanced Systems and Robotics at Sorbonne Université. My work sits at the intersection of perception, estimation, and multi-robot coordination, and I am looking to continue it through a PhD in robotics perception.

I care about robotics that helps us understand and protect the natural world, which is part of what draws me to field and environmental applications. Outside research I read about pure mathematics and deep learning, and I am always glad to talk about any of it.
